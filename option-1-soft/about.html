About Us

A dance-fitness studio built around one instructor's full attention.

Dance with Pri is a dance and fitness studio in Bangalore, founded by Pri to make dance-based workouts feel welcoming instead of intimidating. Every class is taught by Pri herself, in small batches, with choreography and conditioning designed to meet dancers exactly where they are.

Our programs span Bollywood, Zumba, Hip-Hop, Freestyle, Kids classes and private bridal choreography — all taught with the same standard: real technique, real results, no pressure.
