About Us

A dance-fitness studio built around one instructor's full attention.

Dance with Pri is a dance and fitness studio in Bangalore, founded by Pri to make dance-based workouts feel welcoming instead of intimidating. Every class is taught by Pri herself, with choreography designed to meet dancers exactly where they are.

"I built this studio for people who think they can't dance. Most of them end up being my favourite students."

— Pri, Founder & Instructor
